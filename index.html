The Problem

Housing insecurity affects millions of people across the United States, but it is often treated as a problem that begins only when someone loses their home. In reality, housing insecurity can begin much earlier, when families struggle to afford rent, fall behind on payments, or have little financial room to handle an unexpected expense. Today, too many people are left without the financial security, knowledge, resources, and support needed to remain securely housed.

Who We Are

The Housing Security Project is a youth-led organization working to revolutionize the culture of financial preparedness and prevent housing insecurity in communities. Through financial education, community initiatives, volunteer projects, and advocacy, we encourage healthy financial habits, strengthen support systems, and help create communities where people are better prepared for financial hardship before it becomes a housing crisis.

Our Approach

01. Build Knowledge

Through financial literacy programs, educational resources, workshops, and community outreach, we teach young people practical skills in saving, budgeting, spending, credit, debt, emergency planning, and housing. We encourage healthy financial habits that help people prepare for unexpected hardship before it becomes a crisis.

02. Build Connection

Through public education, personal stories, community conversations, and awareness initiatives, we encourage people to view saving, planning ahead, and preparing for unexpected hardship as habits worth building rather than actions taken only when a crisis occurs.

03. Build Action

Through chapters, volunteer projects, and advocacy, we spread HSP's mission to students and communities, supporting local initiatives and working to develop and pass policies that strengthen housing and financial security.

About Us

Empowering young people and communities to revolutionize financial preparedness.

Our Vision

A future where financial preparedness is part of everyday life, where people are encouraged to save, plan ahead, and build the financial security needed to withstand unexpected hardship. We want to help create a culture of self-reliance where people are better prepared before a financial crisis becomes a housing crisis, while ensuring that those facing circumstances beyond their control have support when they need it.

Our Mission

Empowering young people and communities to revolutionize the culture of financial preparedness by building financial security, developing healthy saving habits, and creating support systems that prevent housing insecurity, so no individual or family faces them alone.

What We Believe

We believe housing security starts before a crisis. Building healthy spending and saving habits, preparing for unexpected expenses, and understanding how to manage money can help people remain financially stable when hardships occur.

We want to help create a culture where saving and financial preparedness are part of everyday life, not something people think about only after a crisis begins. At the same time, we believe strong community and public support systems are essential for people facing circumstances beyond their control.

“I used to think that financial planning was something I would worry about when I was older. The Housing Security Project helped me understand that the habits I build now can shape my financial security later. Learning how to budget, save, prepare for unexpected expenses, and understand financial choices gave me skills that I can carry into adulthood. Starting early makes financial security feel less like a distant goal and more like something I can actively build.”

- Youth Participant

“Facing eviction can make you feel like no one is listening. The Housing Security Project helped me understand my options, find resources, and feel like my voice mattered. Knowing that our experiences are also being shared with legislators made me feel like we’re not just dealing with these problems, but we’re also helping create change.”

- Community Member

What is an HSP Chapter?

We believe young people have an important role to play in understanding housing insecurity and creating change in their communities.

Through our chapter program, we support young people as they lead projects focused on financial education, housing awareness, community support, and advocacy. Our chapters bring HSP's mission into their own communities through educational programs, fundraisers, service projects, and other local initiatives.

Chapter leaders will do:

  • Draft legislation and policy proposals
  • Conduct extensive policy research
  • Gather community perspectives and stories
  • Prepare supporting materials for local governments
  • Connect with policymakers and community organizations
  • Advocate fiercely for our proposals

Share Your Story

Every story deserves to be heard.

"By sharing these stories, we work to reduce stigma, build understanding, and remind communities that housing insecurity can affect anyone."

We believe that behind every experience with housing insecurity is a person and a story that deserves to be heard. Through HSP, we invite individuals and families affected by housing insecurity, eviction, or financial hardship to share their experiences and help others better understand the realities behind these issues.

We respect the privacy of everyone who shares their story with us. Stories may be shared anonymously, and we will not publicly identify or share personal information without permission.
